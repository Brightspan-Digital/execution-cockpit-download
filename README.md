## What it does

* Hotkey and on-screen order entry, including dollar or percent sizing
* Broker-side or client-side protective stops and take-profit targets
* Partial exits (25 / 50 / 75 %), FLATTEN and EMERGENCY EXIT
* Account reconciliation and session recovery after a restart

It places and manages the orders you choose. It does not predict prices, give trading signals or advice, or make
trading more profitable.

**Your positions are always yours to manage:** a missing or expired license only stops new entries and adding to
a position. Closing, stops, targets, FLATTEN and EMERGENCY EXIT always work.
