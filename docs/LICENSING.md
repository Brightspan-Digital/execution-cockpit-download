# Licensing and activation (Public Preview)

**Testing the Public Preview is free. No payment is required, and none is possible.** Use the free trial or a
promotional code.

> **Your positions are always yours to manage.** Without a license, only new entries and adding to a position are
> blocked. Closing, reducing, stops, targets, FLATTEN and EMERGENCY EXIT always work, whatever the license says.

The header of Execution Cockpit shows a small **license chip** (its tooltip says what your license is and when it
ends) and an **Account** button that opens the **License window**.

## Option A: free 7-day trial (no account needed)

1. Start Execution Cockpit and click **Account** to open the License window.
2. Click **Start free trial on this computer**.
3. The license chip now shows the trial. Hover over it to see when the trial ends.

There is one trial per computer. A trial ends exactly at its end time, with no grace period. The preview service
limits how many trials can be started from one internet connection per day: if it says the limit was reached,
try again the next day.

## Option B: promotional code

If you received a promotional code (for example as a reviewer or tester):

1. In the License window click **Open my account**. The preview licensing website opens. It calls itself
   *Staging*: that is this preview's licensing service. Its note that licenses "do not activate the released app"
   means the later commercial release; trials and promotional codes from it work with this Public Preview.
2. Create an account with your e-mail address and a password, then confirm your e-mail address. If the website
   says automatic e-mail is not available, follow the instructions it shows.
3. Enter your promotional code on the website. It adds the time stated with the code.
4. On the website open **Overview → your license → activation key** and copy the key.
5. In the License window paste the key and click **Activate**.

**Check now** asks the service for your current license at once.

## Moving to another computer

Each license allows a set number of computers (see **Devices** in the License window). To move: on the old
computer click **Deactivate this computer**, then activate on the new one. **Reset licensing on this computer…**
forgets the license and this computer's device key. It does not change your settings or positions.

## No payments in the Public Preview

The preview licensing website is a **test environment**. Its Billing page shows the plans, but **no payment method
is open**: it cannot create a payment request or show a payment address.

* **Never send real Bitcoin, USDT or any other money for a preview license**, whatever a page or a person says.
  Anything labelled *TESTNET* on that website is **TEST ONLY**.
* Paid plans (39.99 USD for 30 days) start with the commercial release, from its own website.
* Trials, promotional time and licenses from the preview do not carry over to the commercial release.

## Good to know

* Execution Cockpit confirms your license in the background and keeps working offline for a limited time.
* If the chip says your computer's clock is earlier than the last trusted time, turn on **Set time automatically**
  in Windows Settings.
* Never share the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ExecutionCockpit\license`, your activation key, your passwords or any
  recovery phrase with anyone, including us.
